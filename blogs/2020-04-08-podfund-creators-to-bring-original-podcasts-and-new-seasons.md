Podcasting is proving to be resilient and purposeful in the midst of our global crisis, and the storytellers from the Podfund portfolio are more active and inspired than ever. Listeners will get to enjoy new content from their favorite shows in the Podfund portfolio: Double Elvis’ Dear Young Rocker will return for season two, this time following Chelsea to college where she continues to shape her identity. Pushkin will publish new seasons of their greatest hits, including Revisionist History , Against The Rules , Solvable , and The Happiness Lab .
