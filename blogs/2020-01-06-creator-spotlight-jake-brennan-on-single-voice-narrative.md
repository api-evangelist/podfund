Creator Spotlight: Jake Brennan on single-voice narrative storytelling and being a podcast front man Jake Brennan is the creator and host of Disgraceland , a music and true crime podcast about musicians and their transgressions. The show launched in February 2018 and has been downloaded millions of times in over 150 countries. Jake’s book, DISGRACELAND: Musicians Getting Away with Murder and Behaving Very Badly , was released in October 2019.
