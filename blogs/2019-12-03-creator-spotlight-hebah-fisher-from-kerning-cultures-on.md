Creator Spotlight: Kerning Cultures’s Hebah Fisher on going from podcast to network Hebah Fisher is the cofounder of Kerning Cultures , the first venture-backed podcast company in the Middle East. A serial entrepreneur and journalist, Hebah previously built microfinance and business education programs in the Gulf and the States. We chatted with Hebah about how and why she started both Kerning Cultures the podcast and Kerning Cultures the company, and what it’s like working across seven time zones.
