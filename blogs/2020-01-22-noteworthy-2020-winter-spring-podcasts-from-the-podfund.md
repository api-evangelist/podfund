Podfund continues to invest in creator-led, audio-first media companies bringing high quality, entertaining stories to listeners everywhere. The upcoming slate from the Podfund portfolio includes 14+ new shows from our creators, exploring everything from love, infidelity, undercover FBI agents, and the right to vote. A number of established shows from Podfund studios are launching new seasons: Pushkin Industries will launch a mini-season of The Happiness Lab in January, and Broken Record will start releasing episodes continuously on January 28.
